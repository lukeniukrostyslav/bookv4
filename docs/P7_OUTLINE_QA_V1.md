# P7 OUTLINE QA — CONTINUITY AND REVEAL AUDIT V1

Date: 2026-09-21

## Audit result

The first outline contains a strong macro structure but has several continuity weaknesses that must be repaired before freeze.

### Finding 1 — Access-record timing contradiction

The story engine says the false access record is created through a correction applied around the disappearance, while Chapter 21 says the correction occurs after the disappearance and changes an earlier record.

**Repair:** the underlying access event is real, but the identity association is corrected on Day +1. The correction retroactively causes the system to display Claire as the person associated with the 8:17 event. This preserves both facts: the physical event happened on Day 0, while the false identity linkage is created after the disappearance.

### Finding 2 — Phone data mechanism is too vague

The earlier architecture says phone information is "interpreted through the same identity/account assumption." That is not sufficiently concrete.

**Repair:** the phone is not literally reassigned to Claire. Instead, a legitimate account/profile correction causes downstream investigators to associate a device/account record with Claire. The raw device event remains real, but the person-to-device attribution is wrong.

### Finding 3 — Transport timestamp wording

A timestamp generated by a downstream process cannot by itself prove a trip occurred. The outline must distinguish event time from ingestion/settlement time.

**Repair:** the transport record is real but the identity attribution and displayed event time are downstream fields. Nora finds that the displayed time is not the vehicle's physical location time.

### Finding 4 — Daniel's access scope

Daniel cannot plausibly control healthcare, building security, phone data and transport systems directly.

**Repair:** Daniel controls only the healthcare identity record. Other systems independently consume or rely on identity information supplied through legitimate verification processes. His power is to create the initial false association and exploit its propagation, not to edit every system.

### Finding 5 — Claire's fate is underspecified

The outline says her fate is confirmed but does not specify what happened.

**Repair:** Claire survives but is injured and deliberately concealed from the official investigation because Daniel's original plan depended on her disappearance being interpreted as voluntary. This permits a satisfying reunion while preserving consequences.

### Finding 6 — Antagonist's physical mistake is underdefined

"Nora creates a situation" is too vague for fair-play.

**Repair:** Nora publicly submits a request that forces Daniel to verify a physical archive item that should not exist under his false timeline. Daniel physically accesses the archive and moves a document/device. An independent camera and access log capture the action, creating proof that cannot be explained by the false administrative timeline.

### Finding 7 — Midpoint breadcrumb needs physical form

The breadcrumb must be memorable and solvable.

**Repair:** Claire leaves a printed copy of a correction-history screen with one timestamp circled and the handwritten note: "Ask what was corrected, not what was entered." The clue points Nora toward correction history rather than content alone.

## Clue coverage audit

All core clues now have planned payoffs:

- 8:17 access record → final timeline reconstruction.
- service corridor footage → physical contradiction.
- phone attribution → dependency discovery.
- transport event/settlement time → timestamp distinction.
- workplace record → inherited identity data.
- old paper record → origin of false association.
- Claire's breadcrumb → method for investigating correction history.
- repeated correction requests → propagation mechanism.
- Daniel's explanation → partial truth used to disguise false association.
- final physical intervention → independent proof.

## Reveal setup audit

The final reveal will have visible setup in Acts I–III:

1. identity association can change independently of physical events;
2. later systems can inherit earlier identity data;
3. correction history contains information not visible in current records;
4. Daniel has legitimate access to the originating healthcare identity system;
5. Daniel reacts specifically when Nora approaches the correction history;
6. physical evidence is more reliable when separated from identity attribution.

## Solve-too-early test

Nora cannot solve the full mystery before Chapter 27 because each early clue has an alternative ordinary explanation. The decisive pattern only appears when she separates:

**physical event → timestamp → identity attribution → downstream record.**

## Pacing audit

Potential exposition-heavy chapters: 10, 17, 23 and 35.

Repair rule: each must contain a confrontation, decision or physical action rather than pure explanation.

## QA decision

**V1 outline: NOT FROZEN.**

Required next action: produce revised V2 outline incorporating the seven repairs above and then run a second continuity/reveal audit.
