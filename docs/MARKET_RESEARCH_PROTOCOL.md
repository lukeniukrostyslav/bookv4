# MARKET RESEARCH PROTOCOL

## Objective

Identify an adult eBook audience with substantial purchasing activity and a commercially viable opportunity.

## Research dimensions

1. Market size and recent trend
2. Country/language
3. Buyer demographics where reliable data exists
4. Genre
5. Subgenre
6. Reader intent
7. Series behavior
8. Price bands
9. Kindle/eBook suitability
10. Competition density
11. Review volume and reader complaints
12. Search/discovery signals
13. Cover conventions
14. Description/blurb conventions
15. Distribution constraints

## Evidence standard

Every material market claim must include:
- source
- publication/update date
- geography
- population/sample where applicable
- what the metric actually measures
- limitations

## Decision rule

Do not select a genre merely because it is large. Select a concept only after comparing:
- audience demand
- buyer intent
- competition
- differentiation space
- production feasibility
- series potential
- distribution feasibility

## Output

The research phase must produce:
- MARKET_OVERVIEW.md
- AUDIENCE_SEGMENTS.md
- GENRE_SHORTLIST.md
- COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md
- COMMERCIAL_OPPORTUNITY.md
- DECISION_RECORD.md
