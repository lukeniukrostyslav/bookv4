# P7 CHAPTER OUTLINE — DRAFT V1

Date: 2026-09-21
Working title: The Record Says She Never Left
Target: 36 chapters + epilogue
Target manuscript: 80,000–90,000 words
Status: draft architecture — requires continuity/reveal QA before freeze

## ACT I — THE IMPOSSIBLE DEPARTURE

### Chapter 1 — The Missing Appointment
Nora waits for Claire to arrive at an appointment and receives no response. Establish Nora's professional habit of trusting documented facts.
Turn: Claire's phone is unreachable.

### Chapter 2 — 8:17 PM
Police establish the official departure time from Claire's building access record.
Turn: Nora sees the access record and realizes the timestamp conflicts with what Claire had told her earlier.

### Chapter 3 — The Service Corridor
Nora obtains building footage. Claire enters a service corridor but there is no corresponding exit.
Turn: first physical contradiction.

### Chapter 4 — The Simple Explanation
Authorities suggest Claire left voluntarily through another route.
Turn: Nora finds no physical route consistent with the official departure.

### Chapter 5 — Claire's Desk
Nora searches Claire's apartment and finds evidence that Claire had been comparing records.
Turn: an old document contains a small unexplained correction.

### Chapter 6 — The First Correction
Nora traces the old correction and finds it was legitimate-looking.
Turn: Claire had marked the correction before disappearing.

### Chapter 7 — The Decision
Nora decides to reconstruct Claire's final day herself.
Turn: she discovers that the official timeline already contains a second discrepancy.

## ACT II — THE TIMELINE THAT CANNOT EXIST

### Chapter 8 — The Phone
Phone location data appears to place Claire outside the building.
Turn: Nora notices the location evidence relies on the same identity/account information as the access record.

### Chapter 9 — The Ride
A transport record appears to show Claire traveling across town.
Turn: the timestamp is generated by a downstream process rather than direct physical observation.

### Chapter 10 — The Workplace
Claire's workplace record appears to corroborate the official timeline.
Turn: Nora learns the workplace system imported identity information from another source.

### Chapter 11 — Three Independent Records
Nora realizes the three "independent" confirmations may share one origin.
Turn: the apparent evidence chain is not independent.

### Chapter 12 — The Family Story
Nora confronts her brother Ethan about Claire's behavior and their family conflict.
Turn: Ethan reveals Claire had recently asked him about an old family document.

### Chapter 13 — The Old File
Nora compares a paper record with a later digital record.
Turn: the same discrepancy existed before Claire's disappearance.

### Chapter 14 — The Supervisor
Nora interviews Mara Lewis, Claire's former supervisor.
Turn: Mara admits Claire was investigating a records anomaly but refuses to say why.

### Chapter 15 — The Breadcrumb
Nora finds Claire's deliberate breadcrumb.
Turn: Claire expected Nora to reconstruct the evidence chain after her disappearance.

### Chapter 16 — The Wrong Question
Nora stops asking "where did Claire go?" and asks "who needed the record to say she went there?"
Turn: this reframes the entire investigation.

### Chapter 17 — The Correction Trail
Nora follows the history of record corrections.
Turn: several corrections trace back to a common administrative process.

### Chapter 18 — Midpoint: Claire Knew
Nora proves Claire had identified the same propagation problem before disappearing.
Turn: someone knew Claire was close to the source.

## ACT III — THE FALSE REALITY

### Chapter 19 — Daniel Mercer
Nora meets Daniel, whose role gives him legitimate knowledge of the records system.
Turn: Daniel gives a perfectly reasonable explanation for the discrepancies.

### Chapter 20 — The Reasonable Story
Nora tests Daniel's explanation against the physical evidence.
Turn: one detail cannot fit his version.

### Chapter 21 — The Building Manager
Victor admits a security correction was made after Claire disappeared.
Turn: the correction appears routine but changes the meaning of an earlier access record.

### Chapter 22 — The Paper Trail
Nora discovers the original paper document behind the first false entry.
Turn: the false identity association existed months earlier.

### Chapter 23 — The Patient Record
Nora connects the identity discrepancy to an old healthcare record.
Turn: Claire's investigation was not about a clerical error; it was about a person's identity being used incorrectly.

### Chapter 24 — The Missing Link
Nora discovers Daniel's involvement in the correction chain.
Turn: motive becomes possible but not yet proven.

### Chapter 25 — The Trusted Witness
Mara appears to confirm Daniel's explanation.
Turn: Nora discovers Mara is repeating a legitimate-looking record without independent verification.

### Chapter 26 — The Pressure
Daniel warns Nora indirectly that continuing the investigation could damage Claire's reputation and Nora's own professional standing.
Turn: Nora realizes Daniel is reacting to specific evidence, not random suspicion.

### Chapter 27 — The Physical Timeline
Nora rebuilds Claire's final night from physical events rather than administrative records.
Turn: the official timeline becomes impossible at one precise point.

### Chapter 28 — The Motive
Nora identifies the original fraud/identity association and understands why Claire had to be silenced.
Turn: Daniel's motive is now clear.

### Chapter 29 — The Trap
Nora attempts to secure the evidence independently.
Turn: Daniel anticipates her move and tries to create a final administrative explanation for the missing evidence.

### Chapter 30 — The Last Correction
Nora discovers that one remaining record is about to be corrected.
Turn: if it changes, the physical evidence will become harder to interpret.

## ACT IV — CORRECTING THE RECORD

### Chapter 31 — The Reconstruction
Nora assembles the complete evidence chain and identifies the first false assumption.
Turn: she realizes she needs Daniel to make one new physical mistake.

### Chapter 32 — The Impossible Request
Nora creates a situation in which Daniel must act physically to protect the false timeline.
Turn: Daniel makes the required intervention.

### Chapter 33 — The Proof
The physical action contradicts the official record.
Turn: Nora obtains independent corroboration.

### Chapter 34 — Claire's Fate
The investigation finally establishes what happened to Claire.
Turn: the emotional truth is more complicated than Nora expected.

### Chapter 35 — The Record Breaks
Investigators and an independent reviewer reconstruct the administrative chain.
Turn: Daniel's role is exposed.

### Chapter 36 — What Really Happened
The full timeline is explained. Claire's evidence is preserved. Nora accepts responsibility for the distance between them without accepting the false family narrative.
Turn: the mystery closes without a sequel cliffhanger.

## EPILOGUE — THE RECORD

Several weeks later, Nora returns to her work.

A routine discrepancy appears in a new case.

She does not assume the record is true or false.

She starts by asking what physical event the record is supposed to describe.

Final thematic note: a record is evidence of a claim about reality, not reality itself.
