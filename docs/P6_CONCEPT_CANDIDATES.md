# P6 COMMERCIAL CONCEPT CANDIDATES — BOOKV4

Date: 2026-09-21

## Purpose

Generate original concepts from the US-first research without declaring a guaranteed bestseller. These are concept candidates, not approved titles.

## Concept 1 — Psychological Thriller

### Working title
**The House That Remembers**

### Reader
High-frequency psychological/domestic thriller reader.

### One-sentence hook
A woman moves into the house where her childhood friend disappeared, only to discover that the house seems to reveal memories that no one else remembers.

### Reader promise
Fast psychological suspense, secrets, unreliable memory, escalating revelations and a satisfying explanation.

### Differentiation
The central mystery is not simply who committed a crime; the house itself becomes the mechanism through which conflicting memories are uncovered.

### Series potential
Medium. Could become an anthology/series around memory-based mysteries.

### Production difficulty
Medium.

### English-market portability
High.

## Concept 2 — Domestic Thriller

### Working title
**The Woman in the Family Photo**

### Reader
Psychological/domestic thriller audience.

### One-sentence hook
A woman finds a decades-old family photograph containing a stranger who looks exactly like her—and everyone in her family insists the woman never existed.

### Reader promise
Immediate mystery, family secrets, escalating paranoia and multiple reversals.

### Differentiation
The visual hook is instantly understandable and highly suitable for cover/blurb positioning.

### Series potential
Low-medium; primarily standalone.

### Production difficulty
Medium.

### English-market portability
Very high.

## Concept 3 — Dark Romance / Thriller Crossover

High.

High.

## Concept 8 — Story-Driven Nonfiction

### Working title
**The Psychology of the Perfect Lie**

### Reader
Popular psychology + narrative nonfiction reader.

### One-sentence hook
Using documented cases and behavioral science, the book examines why intelligent people believe convincing lies—and how deception works in everyday life.

### Reader promise
Narrative case studies + psychology + practical understanding.

### Differentiation
Story-first structure rather than a generic self-help manual.

### Series potential
High as a broader psychology-of-everyday-life series.

### Production difficulty
High because claims and cases require rigorous sourcing.

### English-market portability
Medium-high.
