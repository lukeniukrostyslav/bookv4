# P7 CHAPTER CARDS V1

## Card standard
Each chapter must advance at least two of: investigation, character, evidence, risk, interpretation, consequential question. Technical material must be dramatized through a character's decision or conflict. Legal/procedural details are intentionally framed as fictional story mechanics and must be fact-checked during manuscript QA.

3. Legal/procedural claims remain fictionalized unless independently fact-checked during manuscript QA.
4. Technical exposition must always be attached to a character conflict, decision, fear, or consequence.
5. Claire's breadcrumb is discoverable before the midpoint and pays off through the final reconstruction.
6. The reader can reconstruct the final mechanism retrospectively from planted evidence.
7. All major mysteries close by Chapter 36; epilogue is thematic only.
8. Manuscript production remains locked until this card set passes the final freeze audit.
