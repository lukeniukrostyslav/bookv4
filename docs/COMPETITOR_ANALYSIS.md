# COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS — BOOKV4

Date: 2026-09-21

## Purpose

Compare the four current commercial lanes before selecting a book concept. This is a market-positioning analysis, not a prediction of sales.

## Evidence snapshot

A 2026 Pubscout analysis of 34,700+ U.S. Kindle listings reports:
- Romance: 6,208 listings; 53.3% KDP; 8,023 average reviews; $9.44 average price.
- Fantasy: 3,422 listings; 62.7% KDP; 9,132 average reviews; $11.72 average price.
- Self-Help: 2,584 listings; 61.6% KDP; 2,350 average reviews; $13.34 average price.
- Mystery: 1,716 listings; 46.6% KDP; 2,210 average reviews; $8.86 average price.
- Thriller: 1,999 listings; 46.3% KDP; 15,762 average reviews; $10.44 average price.

The same analysis notes that thriller has the highest average review count in its sample and the lowest KDP share among the listed major genres, suggesting a particularly established competitive field for new indie titles.

Third-party 2026 category research also shows substantial commercial activity in fantasy, romantasy and suspense, but its sales estimates are not official Amazon sales figures and should be treated as directional rather than verified revenue.

## Competitive implications

### Romance
Demand and indie participation are both substantial. The main strategic risk is entering a broad, generic romance lane without a sharply defined reader promise, trope stack, setting, or crossover hook.

### Fantasy / romantasy
Indie participation is strong and average price in the Pubscout sample is higher than romance. Competition is also substantial, and fantasy generally requires more worldbuilding and production effort. A commercially viable concept would need a clear subgenre identity and strong differentiation.

### Mystery / thriller / suspense
The category has strong reader engagement signals, but the Pubscout sample shows very high average review counts and a lower KDP share than romance/fantasy/self-help. This makes the competitive barrier important to account for. A focused subgenre or distinctive hook would be preferable to a generic thriller.

### Self-help / popular psychology
The Pubscout sample shows strong KDP participation and a higher average price than romance, mystery and thriller. The key risk is credibility: the book must offer a specific, useful promise supported by sound reasoning and clearly distinguish itself from generic advice.

## Current conclusion

No genre is locked yet.

The next decision gate should compare concrete titles and subgenres on:
1. buyer intent,
2. competitive density,
3. review burden,
4. differentiation space,
5. production feasibility,
6. series / follow-on potential,
7. international translation potential,
8. cover and metadata clarity.

This prevents choosing a genre from headline popularity alone.

## Source quality note

The strongest evidence in this file is the Pubscout listing analysis. Other category-revenue estimates are third-party estimates with methodology limitations and are not treated as official Amazon sales data.
