# P5 DEEP COMPETITOR RESEARCH — BOOKV4

Date: 2026-09-21

## Scope

This pass deepens the competitor gate using current Italian-market and BookTok signals. It does not select a winning genre.

## Current evidence

### Italian Kindle / romance

A third-party August 25, 2026 analysis of Amazon.it Kindle romance reported a leading group including:
- *Bad Romance. Illusione proibita* — Claudia Castiello
- *Il marito senza cuore* — series entry
- *Un'estate dopo l'altra* — Carley Fortune
- *The Campus* series titles
- *Il duca dannato* — series entry

The same analysis describes recurring romance positioning around specific tropes such as billionaire, mafia, age-gap and secret pregnancy. This is directional market observation, not official Amazon sales data.

### Italian romantasy / fantasy

September 2026 Italian release coverage shows continued density of romantasy/fantasy launches, including:
- *West of Wicked*
- *City of Mirth and Malice*
- *Tagliatele la testa*
- *I hope this email finds you in hell*
- *Come farsi lasciare da un goblin in dieci giorni*
- *L'ultimo drago d'oriente*
- *Bloody Black*
- *Queen of Faces*
- *Den of Liars*

The pattern is notable for repeated combinations of fantasy + romance, dark retelling, folklore, cozy fantasy, demons, pirates and other highly specific hooks.

### Recognized Italian self-publishing signal

Amazon's January 2026 announcement of the Italian Kindle Storyteller 2025 winner identifies *Il trono del lupo* by Gea Petrini as a fantasy-romance with Nordic/medieval inspiration. Amazon's jury highlighted the balance of fantasy action and romantic tension, narrative structure and three-dimensional characters. This demonstrates that fantasy-romance is not only a social-media trend but also appears in a major Italian self-publishing competition.

## Competitive patterns to carry into concept design

1. **Specific hook beats generic genre labeling.**
   Competitors frequently combine a broad genre with a precise hook: dark retelling, enemy romance, mafia, folklore, pirates, demons, cozy fantasy, etc.

2. **Series architecture is common.**
   Current lists contain multiple series entries and sequels. A new book should therefore be evaluated for both standalone satisfaction and credible continuation potential.

3. **Romantasy has substantial discovery activity but heavy competition.**
   September release coverage contains many distinct romantasy/fantasy launches, while BookTok tracking also shows active fantasy/romance categories.

4. **A crossover concept can widen the promise but increases execution requirements.**
   Combining romance + fantasy/thriller can create a clearer emotional and plot promise, but the manuscript must satisfy both genre expectations.

## Research limitation

Current public sources do not provide a clean, comparable dataset of unit sales for these individual titles. Amazon rankings are relative indicators and should not be treated as direct sales counts.

## Next gate

Move from competitor description to **opportunity-gap analysis**:
- identify repeated hooks;
- identify overused combinations;
- identify underserved combinations;
- formulate 5–7 original reader promises;
- test each promise against competition, production complexity and series potential.

No concept is locked until this gate is documented.
