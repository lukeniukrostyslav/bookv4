## Amazon/KDP structural evidence

Amazon states that Sales Rank is based on customer activity relative to other books and is not an accurate standalone measure for comparing books across categories. Each format has its own Kindle rank, while category ranks aggregate activity in different ways. citeturn0search9

## What this means for BOOKV4

The evidence supports testing **specific subgenres and reader promises**, not merely broad genres.

The next research pass should extract for each candidate subgenre:
- 10–20 comparable titles;
- price and format where available;
- publication date;
- review-count signal;
- series vs standalone;
- recurring tropes or problem/promise;
- cover conventions;
- blurb structure;
- reader complaints and unmet needs;
- evidence of social discovery;
- differentiation opportunities for an original title.
