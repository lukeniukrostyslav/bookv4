# P7 BOOK ARCHITECTURE — INITIAL BLUEPRINT

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: P7 STARTED
Market: United States first
Concept: Concept 9 — working title "The Record Says She Never Left"

## Architecture objective

Build a commercial standalone psychological mystery/thriller with a clear evidence engine, escalating human stakes, fair-play clues and a complete ending.

The architecture is a blueprint, not the final chapter list. Chapter-level beats will be validated before manuscript production.

## Core story equation

**Missing sister + impossible administrative timeline + physical contradiction + investigation into who needed the false record.**

## Four-act macro structure

### Act I — The Impossible Departure

Purpose: establish Nora, Claire, their damaged relationship, the disappearance and the first contradiction.

Target outcome: the reader understands the mystery before the book becomes technically complex.

Major beats:
1. Nora's professional relationship with records is established.
2. Claire fails to appear for an important appointment.
3. Nora learns that Claire apparently left her building.
4. Physical/security evidence contradicts the departure.
5. Nora discovers Claire had recently been investigating an old record discrepancy.
6. Authorities prefer the simpler official timeline.
7. Nora decides to reconstruct the timeline herself.

### Act II — The Timeline That Cannot Exist

Purpose: turn one contradiction into a system of contradictions.

Major beats:
1. Nora checks independent records.
2. Each system appears individually plausible.
3. Combined timestamps become impossible.
4. Nora finds a second discrepancy predating the disappearance.
5. Claire's prior behavior is reinterpreted.
6. Nora realizes someone may have been preparing the record before the disappearance.
7. Nora's own credibility becomes vulnerable.
8. Midpoint: Claire knowingly left a breadcrumb for Nora.

### Act III — The False Reality

Purpose: reveal the human architecture behind the false timeline and increase personal risk.

Major beats:
1. Nora traces the earliest false entry.
2. The apparent conspiracy narrows to a small number of people.
3. Nora discovers how legitimate corrections propagated between systems.
4. A trusted person provides a misleading interpretation.
5. Nora finds the physical location where the false timeline cannot survive.
6. Antagonist attempts to force Nora into accepting the official version.
7. Nora recognizes that correcting one record alone is insufficient.
8. Nora constructs the complete bottom-up timeline.

### Act IV — Correcting the Record

Purpose: deliver fair-play resolution, physical proof and emotional closure.

Major beats:
1. Nora identifies the precise point where the official timeline became impossible.
2. Antagonist is forced into one new physical action.
3. That action contradicts the established record.
4. Nora secures independent corroboration.
5. Claire's fate is resolved.
6. Antagonist's objective and method are exposed.
7. Nora confronts her guilt about Claire.
8. Final image reframes the meaning of a record: documentation can preserve truth, but it cannot replace it.

## Evidence architecture

Every major act must contain:
- one visible contradiction;
- one clue the reader can interpret;
- one human consequence;
- one new question.

Evidence should move from simple to complex:

1. access record;
2. security footage;
3. phone/location data;
4. payment/transport record;
5. workplace or institutional record;
6. witness account;
7. old record connected to Claire;
8. origin of the false entry;
9. physical proof that defeats the administrative timeline.

## Reader knowledge rule

The reader must never be asked to accept a final explanation based on information that was impossible to infer.

The final reveal may be surprising, but the underlying evidence must be visible in retrospect.

## Character architecture

### Nora Bennett
- protagonist;
- records/claims specialist;
- trusts documentation;
- emotionally guilty about distance from Claire;
- learns to distinguish documentation from truth.

### Claire Bennett
- missing sister;
- active investigator before disappearance;
- emotionally independent;
- leaves intentional breadcrumb for Nora.

### Principal antagonist
- must be developed as a human being with a concrete objective;
- cannot be an omnipotent hacker or abstract organization;
- must have a believable reason for constructing the false timeline.

### Supporting cast

Limit the principal suspect field to a manageable number so the book remains commercially readable rather than becoming a procedural catalog.

## Chapter architecture rule

Each chapter should accomplish at least two of the following:
- advance investigation;
- reveal character;
- introduce/reframe evidence;
- increase risk;
- change the protagonist's understanding;
- create a consequential question.

No chapter should exist solely to explain a database, police procedure or technical system.
