# AUDIENCE SEGMENTS — INITIAL MAP

## Segment A — Younger adult digital-heavy readers
Evidence: U.S. e-book reading was 41% among ages 18–29 in Pew's October 2025 survey.
Potential relevance: romance, romantasy, fantasy, thriller.

## Segment B — Adults 30–49
Evidence: U.S. e-book reading was 33% in Pew's October 2025 survey.
Potential relevance: romance, thriller, self-help/popular psychology.

## Segment C — Adults 50–64
Evidence: U.S. e-book reading was 26%.
Potential relevance: thriller, mystery, historical fiction, nonfiction.

## Segment D — College-educated adult readers
Evidence: 42% reported reading an e-book in the prior 12 months.
Potential relevance: broad; must be segmented by genre and buying behavior.

## Important limitation

These are reading-population segments, not confirmed high-spending buyer segments. Purchase frequency and willingness to pay must be researched separately before audience selection.
